# Privacy Policy

Your privacy is taken very seriously. The **discord-nse-data-bot** is designed with a "privacy-first" approach.

## 1. Information Collection
We collect very little information, and only what is strictly necessary for the development and operational maintenance of the bot:
*   **Command Usage:** We may log when a command is used (e.g., `/get_oi_change`) to monitor bot performance and identify bugs.
*   **Discord Metadata:** Standard metadata provided by the Discord API (such as User IDs or Server IDs) may appear in system logs during command execution.

## 2. Use of Information
Any information collected is used solely for:
*   Debugging and improving the bot's functionality.
*   Ensuring the stability of the service.

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion
To further protect your privacy, we have strict data retention policies:
*   **Weekly Deletion:** All operational logs are automatically deleted every week.
*   **Reboot Deletion:** In addition to weekly cycles, logs are cleared whenever the server hosting the bot is rebooted.
*   **No Long-Term Storage:** We do not maintain any long-term databases of user interactions or personal data.

## 4. Third-Party Services
The bot interacts with the Discord API. Your use of Discord is subject to [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy). We do not sell or share any data with third parties.

## 5. Data Security
We implement standard security measures to protect the limited data that passes through our logs during their short retention period.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices. We encourage users to check this file periodically.

## 7. Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please refer to the project's [GitHub repository](https://github.com/siriusbrightstar/discord-nse-data-bot).
